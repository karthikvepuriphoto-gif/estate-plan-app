# Privacy Policy (DRAFT – have counsel review before publishing)
_Last updated: [date]_ · Estate Plan Drafter ("the app") · Contact: [email]

**What we access.** The app asks Google for two permissions: `drive.file` (only files and folders the app itself creates, or that you explicitly pick) and `userinfo.email` (your email address, to label your answers and share a folder with your spouse when you ask). It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

**Where your data lives.** Your answers are stored in a Google Sheet, and your drafts in Google Docs, inside a folder named "Estate Plan Drafts" in **your own** Google Drive. The developer runs no server or database and receives no copy. The GitHub Pages preview stores answers only in your browser's localStorage.

**Sharing.** Nothing is shared unless you click "Share with spouse", which grants that one email address edit access to your folder. We do not sell, rent, or use your data for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Deletion.** Delete the folder from your Drive, and revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

**Children.** Not directed to children under 13. **Changes.** Posted here with a new date.
